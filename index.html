About Me

Hi, I am Zixian Fang (方子贤). I am in the doctoral stage of the integrated master’s and doctoral program in Management Science and Engineering at the School of Economics and Management, Shanghai Maritime University. I received my bachelor’s degree in Logistics Management from Jiangxi Science and Technology Normal University.

I study how shipping networks break down and recover. My research brings together maritime network analysis, large language models (LLMs), and multi-agent reinforcement learning to understand how local disruptions spread and how carriers and ports respond.

  • Network dismantling with reinforcement learning. I explore how agents can learn which ports or routes to disrupt, and in what order, in a simulated shipping network. My aim is to identify which sequences trigger cascading failures and how the set of critical ports changes as disruptions accumulate.
  • Reconstructing real shipping disruptions in simulation. I investigate how LLMs can extract event timelines, affected ports and routes, and operating constraints from reports of actual disruptions. I aim to use those scenarios to simulate decisions by carriers and ports, such as rerouting vessels and handling diverted cargo, and test whether the simulated delays and recovery match what was observed.
